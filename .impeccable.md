## Design Context

### Users
Startup founders and CTOs (seed to Series A) looking for a technical consultant who can ship. They arrive with a specific problem — stalled MVP, AI demo that won't productionize, compliance blocking enterprise sales. Context: evaluating whether to hire this person for a short-term engagement.

### Brand Personality
**Direct. Technical. Ships.**
- Voice: confident without being cocky, technical without jargon-dumping
- Tone: "I've done this before, here's what I'll do for you"
- Emotional goal: visitor should feel "this person ships" + "I need to talk to this guy"

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Style**: Neo-brutalist with terminal/hacker influence
- **Theme**: Dark primary, light secondary. High contrast. Neon green (#00ff88) accent.
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk (display), JetBrains Mono (body/mono)
- **Characteristics**: Thick borders, sharp corners (0px radius), offset box shadows, no gradients, no glassmorphism
- **Anti-references**: Generic SaaS landing pages, Tailwind templates, glassmorphism portfolios
- **Differentiator**: Animated canvas grid backgrounds, terminal typing animation

### Design Principles
1. **Content earns attention** — every section must justify its existence in the scroll
2. **Show, don't decorate** — numbers, metrics, and direct statements over ornamental design
3. **Urgency over comfort** — tight spacing, direct copy, clear CTAs that create forward momentum
4. **Brutalist honesty** — exposed structure, thick borders, no softening. The design reflects the work ethic.
5. **One path forward** — every scroll should move toward the CTA. No dead ends, no detours.
